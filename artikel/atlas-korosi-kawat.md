### KR-17

- **Original sources:** [ISO 1461:2022 — fabricated articles](https://www.iso.org/standard/81435.html), [ISO 14713-2:2019 — design for hot-dip galvanizing](https://www.iso.org/standard/71136.html), and [ISO 1460:2020 — gravimetric coating-mass method](https://www.iso.org/standard/79581.html)
- **Purpose for this article:** Separate after-fabrication hot-dip galvanizing from continuously galvanized sheet/wire/mesh, automatic pipe/tube plants, hardware standards, and extra paint layers.
- **Safe grounded facts:** The same word `galvanis` can refer to materially different base product, coating route, and governing standard. A test method's scope does not state that an actual coating passed.
- **Limits:** Do not transfer ISO 1461 values or acceptance rules to sheet, wire, mesh, pipe, fasteners, or Indonesian procurement without the applicable full standard and order. Resolve G-01/G-05/G-09.

- **Purpose for this article:** Provide practical questions for galvanizer coordination, coating inspection, transport damage, and repair.
- **Safe grounded facts:** Enclosed/overlapped geometry, vent/drain paths, lift orientation, welds, material chemistry, surface contamination, handling, appearance expectations, inspection, and repair interact. Final hole size/location and structural effects require the responsible designer and galvanizer.
- **Limits:** Do not import AGA/ASTM dimensions, acceptance, or repair values into an Indonesian project. Obtain the actual galvanizer's written review, governing standard, product data, approved drawings, and coating specification through G-05/G-09/G-10.

### KR-29

- **Original sources:** [ISO steel-wire, wire-rope, and link-chain catalogue](https://www.iso.org/ics/77.140.65/x/), [ASTM steel standards and current wire/fence families](https://store.astm.org/products-services/standards-and-publications/standards/steel-standards.html), [ISO 2232:2022 wire for specified wire ropes](https://www.iso.org/standard/83194.html).
- **Purpose for this article:** Create a defensible taxonomy for drawn wire, welded/woven mesh, reinforcement, fencing, gabions, expanded metal, stainless wire, rope, seals, and conductors.
- **Safe grounded facts:** Wire used to manufacture a rope is not the finished rope; a mesh panel is not a reinforcement design; chain-link, welded mesh, hardware cloth, gabion mesh, expanded metal, barbed wire, and rope have different evidence chains.
- **Limits:** Do not infer grade, strength, coating, weld quality, opening, capacity, conformity, or suitability from a trade name, visual appearance, nominal diameter, or generic certificate.

### KR-35

- **Original sources:** [ASTM steel standards catalogue including stainless and coated wire families](https://store.astm.org/products-services/standards-and-publications/standards/steel-standards.html), [ISO 12944-2 corrosion environments](https://www.iso.org/standard/64834.html), [ISO 14713-1 zinc-coating guidance](https://www.iso.org/standard/72238.html).
- **Purpose for this article:** Ground carbon/stainless wire, zinc/Zn-Al/aluminium/polymer coatings, forming/welding effects, dissimilar contacts, exposure, contamination, storage, and repair.
- **Safe grounded facts:** Stainless, galvanized, coated, or anti-rust is not a complete specification or service-life claim; an individual wire coating result may not describe welds, edges, fittings, frames, or the installed system.
- **Limits:** Require grade/batch identity, product standard, coating record, fabrication history, environmental/chemical data, compatibility review, inspection method, and product-specific repair guidance.
