## Cross-catalog fact bank

- Wire diameter and material name do not define the product: grade, cold work, coating, construction, mesh/strand geometry, joining, intended system, and acceptance evidence also matter.
- Wiremesh reinforcement, fence mesh, chain-link, hardware cloth, gabion mesh, expanded metal, barbed wire, wire rope, sealing wire, and electrical conductor are different evidence families.
- A product certificate never proves structural layout, posts/foundations, hydraulic/geotechnical works, lifting configuration, security process, cable system, or installed acceptance.
- Welded reinforcement requires design, placement, support/cover, inspection, and approved substitution; sheet size or mass alone does not prove performance.
- Gabion wire/coating evidence is separate from foundation, filter/drainage, fill, hydraulic/geotechnical design, installation, and monitoring.
- Wire-rope breaking force is not a sling working-load rating, and crane, elevator, barrier, guy, and lifting applications require different evidence.
- Stainless or coated does not prove grade, coating class, corrosion life, weld/edge/fitting condition, chemical compatibility, or complete-system performance.
- Current Indonesian law, the governing full standard, project design, manufacturer instructions, and competent inspection remain gates for exact numbers and safety decisions.
