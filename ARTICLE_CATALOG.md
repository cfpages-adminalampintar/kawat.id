# Article Catalog — kawat.id

Scope: 108 planned Indonesian-language assets across 18 parent topics. Every brief has one primary job and an explicit same-domain boundary. The catalog plans complete coverage; it does not authorize bulk publication.

Domain boundaries: Kawat.id owns wire-product knowledge and buying support. Existing `/services` and `/kawat-*` routes own stock, delivery, contact, and quotation intent. Site-specific structural, hydraulic, lifting, electrical, fence-load, corrosion, and installation decisions remain with appropriately qualified professionals.

Catalog statistics: **18 topics; 108 briefs; 6 briefs per topic; 12 P0, 51 P1, 34 P2, and 11 P3.** Wave W1 is the bounded twelve-asset launch cluster; later waves are staged by system and lifecycle.

Priority legend: **P0** launch/safety foundation; **P1** important decision or risk control; **P2** depth and practical support; **P3** specialist/long-tail depth requiring evidence.
